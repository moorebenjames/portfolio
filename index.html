About

CDP is a product development consultancy for startups and corporates. I've worked on about 20 client engagements, nearly all medical devices and surgical robots, and been programme lead on 4. The biggest was a 20-person optical medtech programme, where I sold and led the first 2 phases and a clinical study, about £1m. Alongside client work I've spent about 1.5 years FTE on sales, pricing, FP&A and governance, including a trustee director seat on CDP's employee ownership trust.

Before CDP I co-founded Volunteer Space, B2B SaaS for volunteer coordination, and ran it as MD for 2 years. Raised a £250k seed round, built a team of 6 and a £1.5m pipeline, then closed it in Dec 2019 without product-market fit. I relied on the technical team for what we could build and when. That's normal in software, but I'm at my best close to the technology, and it's one reason I went back to engineering.
