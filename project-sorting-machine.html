Automating Quality Control

A feasibility study and design initiative to automate the manual quality inspection and sorting of layer pads — the cardboard separators placed between layers of beverage cans during packaging. The manual process was slow, labour-intensive, and inconsistent.

Project Objectives

  • Study feasibility of a fully automated sorting solution
  • Increase production throughput by 250%
  • Remove need to outsource further labour
  • Reduce labour costs and improve quality consistency
  • Achieve payback period of 18 months or less

My Work

  • Analyzed and evaluated existing manual quality inspection processes in detail
  • Developed multiple machine concepts and functionalities in CAD
  • Worked with external turnkey providers to source and evaluate equipment
  • Evaluated electronic sensory equipment for detecting health, safety, and quality issues
  • Collaborated with software and hardware specialists on integrated systems
  • Produced comprehensive feasibility report with full implementation plan
  • Delivered formal presentation to senior leadership to secure approval

Business Case & Results

Financial Case

Estimated annual savings: £300,000
Capital expenditure: £150,000
Estimated ROI: 6 months

Put together complete implementation plans for the company to execute. Left shortly after the presentation, so implementation status is unknown — but the business case was compelling and the plans were fully developed and ready to proceed.
