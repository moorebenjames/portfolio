Titan Medical single-port robot

The one I can talk about publicly: Titan Medical's single-port robot, developed with CDP. I designed the set-up arm, from load analysis and component selection through to testing the Alpha build.

The system

A single-port robot competing with the da Vinci SP, for single-incision or natural orifice surgery: 3 instruments and a camera through one port, with a smaller, lighter footprint to fit more operating rooms.

My work

Structural and mechanical design

  • Designed structural components for stability and precision during procedures.
  • Built the central drive unit that powers and controls the instruments.
  • Designed telescopic set-up arms with failsafe mechanisms.
  • Optimised degrees of freedom and motion types for weight and function

User experience

  • Designed status lighting to give surgeons feedback.
  • Tech landscaping and competitor analysis for design elements
  • Worked on an endoluminal robot from scratch, starting from the requirements.

Concept work on other robots

  • Ran concept brainstorms with clients on new surgical instrument designs.
  • Led concept-phase architecture evaluations and proposed the lead architecture.

Now

Since Sep 2026 I'm technical lead on the systems architecture of a new robotics programme at CDP, proposing the lead design after human factors evaluation with surgeons.
